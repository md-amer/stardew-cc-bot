# Blindspots found, and their fixes

Each entry: what went wrong → root cause → systemic fix (so it can't recur).

1. **Fished the Standard farm pond for an hour.** It only yields trash (wiki: Fish → Farm Pond).
   Cause: acted on an assumption instead of a verified fact.
   Fix: every new activity or location choice gets a wiki check first (`wiki.py search`). NOT YET DONE: `FACTS.md` was planned but never created.
2. **A stray script kept controlling the game.** It cast onto land 12 times and burned 250 stamina.
   Cause: `pkill -f "act.run(f)"` treated the parentheses as regex, so nothing was killed.
   Fix: single-controller lock (pidfile in `act.run`, `/tmp/claude-1000/stardew_agent.pid`; a new run kills the old PID).
3. **The same failure repeated with no stop.** no_cast happened 12 times in a row.
   Fix: circuit breaker. PARTLY DONE: only fishing has one (see 23); other routines still lack it.
4. **Game clock ran while I was thinking or debugging.** Hours of game time were lost.
   Fix: dead-man switch in the mod. If no task is running and no command arrives for 5 s (2 s fired mid-routine), it opens the game menu (clock stops, just like a player pausing).
5. **Backpack full mid-fishing.** Fish were trashed.
   Fix: chest by the shipping bin, and a pre-trip check of at least 5 free slots. Fish are never trashed; junk is swapped out first.
6. **Watering can ran dry silently.** 22 crops stayed dry.
   Fix: `water_all` re-checks dry tiles, refills and retries.
7. **choose_plot reused planted tiles.** Seeds appeared "planted" but weren't.
   Fix: exclude tiles that have crops, and verify a crop exists after planting.
8. **Max-power cast on small water lands on the shore.**
   Fix: the fish command takes a cast power; Python picks it from the water extent in the facing direction.
9. **Walker pressed two keys near corners** and snagged on the porch.
   Fix: move along the dominant axis only.
10. **Status checks hidden by grep.** Errors went unseen.
    Fix: every run logs to `logs/*.log` in full.
11. **Arrived before opening hours.** The CC cutscene window is 8:00–13:00 and Pierre opens at 9:00 (wiki). I arrived at 7:00 and 8:20.
    Fix: every timed action checks its wiki hours first (`cc_unlock` waits for 8:00; shop visits need ≥ 9:00 and not Wednesday).
12. **Mail never read.** The Wizard's door stayed locked because the invitation letter wasn't read.
    Fix: `read_mail` is the first step of every day.
13. **Game crashed.** A killed client made HttpListener throw "Broken pipe" on a worker thread, which kills the process.
    Fix: all socket reads and writes are wrapped. Lost Day 6 progress up to that point (reloaded the morning save; crash recovery, not scumming).
14. **Hand-edited progress.json lost the chest counts.** Chest bookkeeping drifted.
    Fix: no counters. `sync_chest` reads the real chest contents every visit.
15. **Cutscene after a warp broke the "wait until free" check** (Marlon, Lewis).
    Fix: every warp ends with a click-through-events loop.
16. **A dialog left open blocked movement** on the next script.
    Fix: `run()` and `walk()` dismiss stray dialogs first.
17. **Seed purchases silently did nothing.** ShopMenu has a 250 ms `safetyTimer` after opening that ignores clicks (found in decompiled code).
    Fix: wait 0.6 s after any shop opens. General rule: when a UI click "does nothing", read the menu's click handler for gates.
18. **Started mining from floor 1 every day.** The lift goes straight to the deepest unlocked floor (Amer spotted this).
    Fix: the mining session starts at the MineElevator, picks the deepest floor, and leaves by lift or up-ladder at positions the game reports (`tileBeneathElevator`/`tileBeneathLadder`).
19. **Bundle donation refused.** The item was dropped onto an already-filled ingredient slot.
    Fix: drop into the first empty slot (index = done count).
20. **Window tiled to 946 px.** Menus were clipped and their buttons sat off-screen.
    Fix: Amer keeps the window maximised on workspace 1, so the restart script does not float or resize it.
21. **Mined with a full backpack,** so stone and ore weren't picked up (Amer spotted it).
    Fix: before mining or fishing, empty to the chest when fewer than 6 (or 4) slots are free. The backpack upgrade is the top money goal.
22. **Went to bed at 3 pm** after mining gave up early.
    Fix: leftover hours go to fishing until about 21:30. Mining tries up to 250 rocks per floor.
23. **Fishing cast onto land 33 times** (the circuit breaker from lesson 3 was written down but never coded).
    Fix: it is now coded. Casts go straight along the longest water run, power steps down, and the spot is abandoned after repeated misses. Rule: a lesson only counts once it is in code.
